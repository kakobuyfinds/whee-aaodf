04 / The comparison framework

What belongs in a useful sheet?

These fields make a Wheebuy spreadsheet comparison easier to revisit, update and understand.

Suggested research fields for your own product shortlist
Field What to record Why it helps
Item & category A descriptive item name, category and source listing URL. Distinguishes similar finds and makes the source easy to revisit.
Selected variation Color, size, material option and any listing-specific identifier. Keeps comparisons tied to the version you actually reviewed.
Measurements Garment dimensions, foot-length guidance or accessory dimensions. Provides more context than a size label alone.
Price & currency Listed item price, original currency and the date checked. Reduces confusion when prices or exchange rates change.
Additional costs Separate estimates for delivery, service fees and applicable import charges. Prevents an item-only price from being mistaken for a final total.
Evidence & status Review references, image observations, unresolved questions and availability check date. Shows what has been verified and what still needs attention.
05 / Details before decisions

A good find needs
more than a good image.

For shoes, compare the listing’s size chart with your foot length and check whether the guidance refers to foot measurements or the shoe itself. For clothing, measure a garment you already like and compare equivalent points.

Accessories need their own checks: dimensions, material descriptions, closure type and care instructions can matter more than the main image. If a detail is missing, keep it marked as unknown.

Photos and quality-check references can help you inspect visible features. They do not independently establish authenticity, durability or future fit.

  • Does the source link open the intended item?
  • Have you recorded the exact color and size?
  • Are the materials described clearly?
  • Can you compare usable measurements?
  • Are delivery costs separate from the item price?
  • Have you checked the seller’s return conditions?
  • Is the date of your last review recorded?

07 / Clear answers

Before you browse.

Direct answers to common spreadsheet research questions.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

A Wheebuy spreadsheet is a product research list associated with searches for Wheebuy finds. A useful list organizes product links, categories, listed prices, sizing notes and review references. This page provides a browsing guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Wheebuy service.

Where do the product category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. General browsing buttons open https://www.cafans.com.

Does a spreadsheet price include shipping?

Do not assume that it does. Record the item price separately from domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm each charge with the relevant provider.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

For clothing, compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. For shoes, compare foot length with the specific listing’s size chart. A size label alone is not enough to establish fit.

Do brand names or illustrations verify authenticity?

No. The brand-themed graphics on this page are editorial illustrations, not photographs of available stock. A spreadsheet entry, product title or image does not establish authenticity, seller authorization or availability.

Is this an official Wheebuy website?

No. This is an independent informational guide that directs browsing links to CAFANS. It is not operated by or endorsed by Wheebuy or the brands mentioned.
